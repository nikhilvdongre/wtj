Bali Honeymoon Escape

6 Days / 5 Nights International Honeymoon Bali, Indonesia

Tour Overview

Escape to the Island of Gods with your partner for a magical 6-day honeymoon experience. Bali offers the perfect blend of romance, culture, and natural beauty — from lush rice terraces and ancient temples to pristine beaches and world-class spa retreats.

Our Bali Honeymoon Escape package is fully customised for couples — with a private pool villa, candle-lit dinners, sunset cruises, and seamless transfers so you can focus entirely on each other.

Duration 6 Days / 5 Nights
Destination Bali, Indonesia
Best For Couples / Honeymoon
Starting From ₹65,000 / person

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Bali — Check-in & Welcome

Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport, Bali. Meet & greet by our local representative. Private transfer to your luxury pool villa. Evening at leisure — explore the villa and relax. Welcome candle-lit dinner by the pool.

Day 2

Ubud — Culture & Nature

Visit the iconic Tegallalang Rice Terraces. Explore Ubud Art Market for local handicrafts. Sacred Monkey Forest sanctuary visit. Evening at Ubud Palace with traditional Kecak dance performance.

Day 3

Temples & Tanah Lot Sunset

Morning visit to Tirta Empul Temple (holy spring water). Afternoon at Beratan Lake and Ulun Danu Temple. Sunset at the famous Tanah Lot sea temple — one of Bali's most iconic sights. Romantic beachside dinner.

Day 4

Seminyak Beach & Spa Day

Leisurely morning at Seminyak beach. Couples spa session (Balinese massage + flower bath) at a luxury spa. Evening sunset cocktails at a clifftop beach club. Dinner at a fine dining restaurant.

Day 5

Nusa Penida Island Day Trip

Full-day trip to Nusa Penida by speedboat. Visit Kelingking Beach (T-Rex cliff viewpoint), Angel's Billabong, and Broken Beach. Snorkeling in crystal-clear turquoise waters. Return to Bali by evening.

Day 6

Departure — Farewell Bali

Morning free for last-minute shopping or a relaxing swim in your private pool. Check-out from villa. Private airport transfer. Departure with beautiful memories of your Bali honeymoon.
